                <h3>
                  <a href="http://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~omkar">Omkar M Parkhi</a> and
                  <a href="http://www.vlfeat.org/~vedaldi">Andrea Vedaldi</a> and
                  <a href="http://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~az/">Andrew Zisserman</a> and
                  <a href="http://www.iiit.ac.in/~jawahar/">C. V. Jawahar</a>
                </h3>
                <br><br>

                <h3>Overview</h3>

                <p>
                We have created a 37 category pet dataset with roughly 200 images for
                each class. The images have a large variations in scale, pose and
                lighting. All images have an associated ground truth annotation of
                breed, head ROI, and pixel level trimap segmentation.
                </p>

                <h3>Downloads</h3>
                <p>
                  The Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset and annotations are
                  roughly 800 MB in size and available for download
                  via BitTorrent with
                  <a href="https://academictorrents.com/">Academic
                    Torrents</a>:
                </p>
                <ul class="ul-large">
                    <li><a href="https://academictorrents.com/details/b18bbd9ba03d50b0f7f479acc9f4228a408cecc1">Torrent (faster download) for dataset and groundtruth</a></li>
                </ul>
                <p>
                  We recommend the use of BitTorrent protocol.  If its
                  use is not possible, the dataset and annotations are
                  also available for download over http as two
                  separate files:
                  <a href="https://thor.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/pets/images.tar.gz">images.tar.gz
                  (dataset)</a> and
                  <a href="https://thor.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/pets/annotations.tar.gz">annotations.tar.gz
                  (groundtruth data)</a>.
                </p>

                <h3>Annotations Examples</h3>
                <img src="pet_annotations.jpg" class="img-fluid">
                
                <p>The following annotations are available for every image in the
                dataset: (a) species and breed name; (b) a tight bounding box (ROI)
                around the head of the animal; and (c) a pixel level
                foreground-background segmentation (Trimap).
                </p>
